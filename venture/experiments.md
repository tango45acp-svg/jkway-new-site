# Experiments

**Rules:**
- Any experiment that needs more than $100 or more than 14 days to its first dollar is rejected.
- Each experiment gets a **kill date**. If it hasn't hit its signal by then, stop it and move the effort elsewhere.
- Time-to-first-revenue (TTFR) runs from the start date to the date the first payment lands in `ledger.csv`.

| ID | Experiment | Channel | Cost | Start | Early signal needed | Kill date | First $ date | TTFR | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Can restart if the founder has time for walk-ins. |
| E2 | Free-check offer in local Facebook groups and Nextdoor | Organic community | $0 | not started | 3 or more inquiries from 5 posts | start + 7 days | — | — | Not started |
| E3 | Personalized cold email to businesses with visible site bugs | Email | $0 | not started | 5% or more reply rate on 100 emails | start + 10 days | — | — | **Primary as of 2026-09-27.** 10 emails a day in one 45-minute sitting, starting Monday 2026-09-28. |
| E4 | LinkedIn and X posts with before/after teardowns of anonymized local sites | Organic content | $0 | after first delivery | 2 or more inbound DMs in 14 days | start + 14 days | — | — | Queued. Needs a real case study first. |

**Kill or scale rule:**
- If every channel misses its signal by day 14 with no sale, the offer is the problem, not the channel. Move to Plan B (below).
- Any channel with 2 or more sales gets double the effort.

**Plan B**, pre-decided so we don't waste time debating it later: a "Google Business Profile Fix" for $99. It's a smaller commitment for the buyer and goes to the same leads through the same channels.

## Rejected ideas (and why)
| Idea | Reason |
|---|---|
| Paid ads | No organic proof of demand yet (a standing rule). |
| Dropshipping or print-on-demand | Needs ad spend to move volume. The first dollar in under 14 days isn't realistic organically. |
| Affiliate or SEO content site | Takes more than 14 days to first dollar (indexing alone takes weeks). |
| Gumroad templates | Possible later, but there's no audience yet, so first sales would take more than 14 days. We'll revisit once E4 builds a following. |
| Trading the seed money | Speculation, not a business. |
